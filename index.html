What it does

One album, everyone

Every guest shoots into the same roll. You get the whole night, not just your corner of it.

Join by code or QR

Guests scan or type a short code. No account to make.

Full quality

Originals, not the squashed copies a group chat gives back.

Download the lot

Take the whole album home when the event is over.

Made for events

A wedding, a bar mitzvah, a trip, a birthday. It closes when you say so.

Questions

Do guests need the app?

They need it to shoot into the album. Joining takes a code or a QR scan, and no account.

How long do the photos stay?

As long as the album is open. Download everything before you close it.

Are the photos public?

No. Only people with the code can see the album.
