One shared camera roll for the whole event.

Everyone at the wedding shot photos. Now they are on forty different phones. GroupSnap puts them all in one place while the party is still happening.

Questions

Do guests need the app?

No. Anyone can open the album in a browser and add photos there. The app is nicer on an iPhone, but it is not required, and neither way asks for an account.

Does the browser version cost anything or need a sign-in?

Neither. You type your name, type the album code, and you are in.

What does it cost?

Nothing for an album of up to 9 people — unlimited photos, unlimited albums. Past 9, the host unlocks the album: $0.99 for that one album forever, or $2.99 every 6 months for every album they host. Guests never pay, and nobody already in an album is ever removed.

How long do the photos stay?

As long as the album is open. Download everything before you close it.

Are the photos public?

No. Only people with the code can see the album. A host can also require guests to be physically at the venue instead of using a code at all.

Can I download the whole album at once?

Not yet. You save photos one at a time — there is no bulk download. If handing someone a folder of everything is the point, say so and we will tell you what to use instead: see the comparison.
