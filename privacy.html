GroupSnap — Privacy

Last updated 2 September 2026

GroupSnap collects as little as it can. This page says exactly what that is.

What is collected

The photos you add to an album, the album you joined, and the display name you chose. No account and no email address.

What is never collected

No advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing sold or shared with data brokers. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

Deleting your data

Email groupsnap@dancykier.com and everything tied to you is removed. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on the device.

Children

GroupSnap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about any of this: groupsnap@dancykier.com
